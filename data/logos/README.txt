Drop your own logo / belt art here to override the built-in emblems.

Name each file by its KEY (extension can be .svg .png .webp .jpg .gif):

  Brands:   raw.png            smackdown.png
  Belts:    world.png  secondary.png  tag.png  cruiserweight.png
            hardcore.png  manager.png
            (or by short name, e.g. rawworld.png, extreme.png)
  PPVs:     wrestlemania.png   royalrumble.png   summerslam.png ...
            (slug = the PPV name, lowercased, letters/numbers only)

Nothing here ships with the app and nothing is downloaded for you — this is
your slot for art you have the right to use. Without a file, the app draws its
own original emblem for that key.
